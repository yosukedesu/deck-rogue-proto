# Physical attack visual direction

User-selected rule (2026-10-26): physical attacks convey force through shadows,
aura, fleeting incomplete silhouettes, directional slash light, compression,
and impact debris/dust. Preserve the actual actors and their equipment. Do not
add oversized literal body parts or solid summoned weapons as visual effects.
This is a design rule, not permission to homogenize different techniques.

## Current scope

- Strike: preserve the actual axe and actor. Its directional luminous cut is
  abstract energy, not an additional illustrated weapon; retain its hot-to-amber
  release and contact registration.
- Stomp: replace the illustrated boot/leg with a looming ground shadow and a
  fleeting, hollow, incomplete aura suggestion just before contact. At contact
  that suggestion breaks into the existing pressure flare, rocks and dust.
  No filled ghost limb or persistent anatomical outline.
- Fire: magic presentation is not changed by this physical-attack rule.

The exact revision64 project remains the comparison baseline. Gameplay remains
single-target with one authoritative hit; camera, saves and unrelated art are
unchanged. A larger shadow/pressure footprint is visual, not additional damage.
Evaluate at actual gameplay scale and in motion before accepting new artwork.
